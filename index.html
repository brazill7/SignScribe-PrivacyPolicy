SignScribe Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains how SignScribe: ASL (“SignScribe,” “we,” or “our”) handles information when you use the app.

Camera and sign recognition

SignScribe uses your device camera when you choose a feature that recognizes signs. Video frames and hand landmarks are processed on your device using Apple’s Vision framework and are not uploaded to SignScribe or RevenueCat by the app. The app does not save camera recordings. You can control camera permission in your device settings.

Information stored on your device

SignScribe stores app preferences and practice information, such as practice progress and custom practice content, in the app’s local storage. This information is not sent to SignScribe servers. It may be included in device backups according to your device settings. Deleting the app may remove locally stored information.

Purchases and RevenueCat

SignScribe uses RevenueCat to load available in-app purchase products, process purchases and restores, and determine whether a purchase provides access to SignScribe+. When the purchase SDK is active, RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user identifier unless the app supplies a user identifier. The app does not require you to create an account.

RevenueCat and Apple may process information needed to operate and verify purchases, which can include the anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction details, subscription and entitlement status, transaction or receipt data, and technical information about the device and app. Apple processes payments; SignScribe does not receive your full payment card number. RevenueCat processes purchase-related information for us under its service terms. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy for more information.

If Apple Family Sharing is enabled for a subscription and a purchaser shares it, Apple may provide eligible family members access to that subscription. SignScribe and RevenueCat process the resulting entitlement information to provide access. Apple does not provide SignScribe with a list of the purchaser’s family members.

How we use information

We use locally stored app information to provide app features and use purchase-related information to provide, restore, and support paid access. We do not sell personal information or use camera frames for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

We share purchase-related information with Apple and RevenueCat as needed to process and verify in-app purchases. We do not operate our own account or analytics server for the app. Apple and RevenueCat handle information according to their own privacy practices and applicable agreements.

Retention and your choices

Practice information remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records according to their policies and legal requirements. You can manage camera permission in device settings and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings. For questions about purchase information associated with RevenueCat, contact us and we will help direct your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. We will post the current version at this page and update the effective date.
